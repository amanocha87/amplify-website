# Amplify Brand & System Guidelines

## Core Philosophy
- **Business First, Technology Second:** Amplify is a business consultancy that uses AI, automation, and digital systems to improve businesses. Technology always supports business strategy—not the other way around.
- **Focus on Outcomes:** Focus on solving business problems and delivering measurable business improvements (e.g., hours saved, systems improved, customer experience enhanced).

## The Amplify Method™
Our proprietary business framework must be a recurring theme:
1. **Access**: Understand the business first. Identify opportunities, bottlenecks, and priorities before recommending technology.
2. **Adapt**: Design practical systems, AI, and automation around the business's existing people, processes, and customers.
3. **Achieve**: Deliver measurable improvements, happier customers, and sustainable business growth.

## Brand Identity & Logos
- **Official Assets:** The uploaded logo assets (both small and large variants with expanding purple waves) are the official Amplify identity. 
- **DO NOT** redesign, replace, simplify, or recreate them. Treat these as permanent.
- The expanding purple waves represent increasing impact (Access → Adapt → Achieve) and symbolize growing intelligence and business growth.

## Brand Personality & Tone
- **Tone:** Premium, Professional, Approachable, Practical, Confident, Consultative.
- **Rules:** 
  - Never feel like a software company.
  - Never feel overly technical or overuse AI terminology.
  - Reinforce that clients are buying a proven business improvement methodology, not simply AI tools.

## Visual Design Style
- **Colors:** Primary palette consists of White, Black, Dark Navy, and Purple (#6E3BFF).
- **Aesthetic:** Minimal, Premium, Apple-inspired, Linear/Stripe-inspired.
- **Spacing:** Generous use of white space.
- **Restrictions:** No unnecessary gradients, no clip-art, no stock branding.
